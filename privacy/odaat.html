Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This privacy policy concerns your use of the mobile application One Day at a Time, which was created by Caleb Payton.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) to external servers. All user data, configurations, and application progress are stored entirely locally on your mobile device's internal storage.

2. Local Storage

The Application requires permission to use your device's local storage to save your user data. Deleting the Application will delete all data stored locally by the app, and this data cannot be recovered by Paytonology.

3. Push Notifications

The Application may request permission to send you push notifications in the future for system updates, reminders, or features. You can opt-out of receiving these communications at any time by changing the notification settings on your mobile device.

4. Third-Party Access

Because your data stays on your device, no third-party services have access to your information through this Application. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to outside parties.

5. Security

We care about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Please be aware that while we provide standard device-level security for data stored on your device, no security system can prevent all potential security breaches if your device itself is compromised.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
